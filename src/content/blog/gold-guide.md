## What is Gaisce?

Gaisce - the President's Award is Ireland's National Youth Award. It's designed to encourage young people (14-25) to be more active, get involved in their communities, and reach their potention. Participants must complete an adventure journey and undertake activities in three challenge areas: community involvement, personal skill, and physical recreation.

There are three levels: Bronze, Silver, and Gold. The Bronze award is the easiest, followed by Silver, then Gold. With the minimum age for Bronze participants being 14, Silver being 15, and Gold being 17, the awards are designed to be completed one after another, but it's not required. Starting with Silver or Gold is possible if you're old enough, although it does require doubling the time spent on one challenge area.

To complete the challenge areas, you have to spend an hour a week on three activities of your choice. For example, volunteering with a charity for *community involvement*, baking for *personal skill*, and running for *physical recreation*. The amount of weeks you have to spend on each section varies by award level. The weeks do not have to be consecutive, but it is easier and quicker if they are. The award is designed to encourage participants to build habits.

The adventure journey is a journey undertaken by the participants' own physical effort (e.g., walking, kayaking, cycling). It is either an expedition or an exploration - an expedition being a journey which focuses on the distance covered, and an exploration one which focuses on the journey. Each award level has a different required time for the adventure journey, from two to four days, and it must be done consecutively. Gaisce also recommends a minimum number of hours of effort for each day of the adventure journey, and a minimum distance.

## What's involved in the **Gold** Gaisce?

The Gold Gaisce involves the usual challenge areas and adventure journey, but also requires a residential project and a portfolio.

52 weeks must be spent on each challenge area at gold level. The adventure journey is 4 days, 3 nights, and should cover ~80km if hiking.

The residential project requires a participant to spend five days in an unfamiliar environment with people who are not their usual companions, working towards a common goal. A participant cannot gain materially from the experience. Examples would include a residential language learning course, residential volunteering (e.g, at a care home), environmental and conservation projects, etc.

The portfolio is a record of your gold award. You keep track of your award using signed record sheets and other key evidence, e.g. photos, maps & plans for your adventure journey or a signed letter for your residential. Each portfolio includes a cover page, a piece on your bronze and silver gaisce (if completed), a piece about your PAL, information and evidence for each of your challenge area activities, a piece about your residential project with evidence, a piece about planning and undertaking your adventure journey, and a conclusion which summarises your gaisce experience. This portfolio is then sent off to Gaisce to be evaluated before you receive your Gold Award.
