#### 15/10

This week, I finished my flower hat for Halloween. This hat was started early in the month, before I began working on my Gold. It's crochet in two colours (according to the pattern - mine ended up being avout five because I was using wool scraps). I had to add a few rows to the end of the pattern, either because it is designed for someone with a smaller head or because I kept losing count and skipping too many stitches.
