## Craft

#### 18/10

I designed a cross stitch pattern for my sister this week. It's going to be a cover for her birthday photo album. It has a lot of pink, because she loves pink. I also put our cats on the letters of her name. I used various sources for the pattern, mainly my mum's cross-stitch books. The letters, border, and animal designs all came from different books. I considering arranging the design on paper but decided against and used an online pattern maker. I stitched the left & bottom border this week, and outlined the top and right borders.

## Walking

#### 26/10

This week's walk took place in the London Science Museum. My guide unit is visiting Pax Lodge, a WAGGS world centre in England, this week. I especially liked seeing the old watches and Charles Babbage's Analytical Engine, as well as a cross stitch sampler with the morse code alphabet.
